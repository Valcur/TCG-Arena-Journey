**The Journey TCG** is a retro RPG trading card game with pixel art illustrations. Build a party of 3 Adventurers and engage in 3v3 Party based combat, inspired by the combat systems of old school RPGs.

Each turn, Adventurers act in Agility order. Agility, HP, Step Back and the turn order are tracked automatically on the board.

All cards from **Brave Exodus** are available, including promos.

**Players:** 2

Card data and images come from the [official The Journey TCG website](https://www.journeytcg.com).
